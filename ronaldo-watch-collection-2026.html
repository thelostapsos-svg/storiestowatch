Cristiano Ronaldo's relationship with luxury watches began as a young Manchester United player reaching for his first Rolex Daytona. It has since evolved into one of the most extraordinary personal collections in professional sport — 54 timepieces with an estimated combined value of $16.8 million. The collection spans diamond-set Rolex references, bespoke Jacob & Co. collaborations, million-dollar Franck Muller tourbillons and a Girard-Perregaux planetary complication that borders on kinetic sculpture. It is, by any measure, the watch collection of a man who has spent twenty years at the very apex of global sport.

This is the definitive guide to CR7's collection in 2026 — every significant piece identified, valued and explained for collectors who want to understand what they're looking at when they see Ronaldo's wrist.

THE CR7 COLLECTION — MOST VALUABLE PIECES

Franck Muller

Cintrée Curvex — Full Diamond Invisible Setting

Ronaldo's most expensive piece. The Franck Muller Cintrée Curvex in full invisible diamond setting uses the serti mysterieux technique — a French jewellery method where stones are set with no visible prongs or metal, creating the illusion of a surface made entirely of diamonds. The result is extraordinary: a watch that appears to be carved from a single block of diamonds. The movement is entirely concealed behind this spectacle.

~$1.5M

Girard-Perregaux

Planetarium Tri-Axial

The most mechanically ambitious piece in Ronaldo's collection and the one most likely to surprise watch enthusiasts. The Planetarium Tri-Axial features three gyroscopic frames rotating on three separate axes — a complication that is partly tourbillon, partly orrery and entirely spectacular. Ronaldo reportedly purchased this piece for its visual drama as much as its mechanical achievement. It is one of the more serious horological acquisitions in any celebrity collection.

~$1.7M

Jacob & Co.

Bugatti Chiron Tourbillon CR7 Edition

The watch created specifically for Ronaldo in collaboration with Jacob & Co. and Bugatti — a piece that combines three of CR7's passions: fine watches, luxury cars and his own brand. The movement is designed to replicate the W16 engine of Ronaldo's Bugatti Chiron, with 16 miniature pistons visible through a sapphire crystal engine block. Press a button and the pistons move. It is maximalist, theatrical and entirely consistent with Ronaldo's aesthetic.

~$1.5M

Rolex

GMT-Master II "Ice" — Ref. 116769TBR

The most famous watch in Ronaldo's collection and historically his most photographed piece. The GMT-Master II Ice is factory-set by Rolex with 30 carats of baguette-cut diamonds across the case, bezel, dial and bracelet in 18ct white gold. At approximately $485,000-$800,000, it remains one of the most expensive watches ever officially offered by Rolex. Ronaldo has been photographed wearing it at Champions League matches, press conferences and on social media countless times.

~$800K

Jacob & Co.

Astronomia Tourbillon

One of twelve Jacob & Co. pieces in Ronaldo's collection. The Astronomia Tourbillon features a four-arm rotating carousel with a miniature Earth globe, a sun-shaped diamond, a tourbillon and the time display — all rotating simultaneously on different axes above the dial. It is one of the most visually dramatic watch mechanisms ever produced and one of the pieces that first attracted Ronaldo to Jacob Arabo's house twenty years ago.

~$750K

Ronaldo's collection is not a random accumulation of expensive objects. It is a coherent statement about maximalism, craftsmanship and the intersection of sport and culture at the highest level.

THE 20-YEAR JACOB & CO. RELATIONSHIP

The most significant thread running through Ronaldo's collection is his two-decade relationship with Jacob Arabo and Jacob & Co. What began as a personal friendship between a rising football star and New York's most famous jeweller has evolved into a formal creative partnership — with Ronaldo co-designing watches that bear his CR7 branding and sell to his own fanbase.

The Flight of CR7 and Heart of CR7 collections — both produced in collaboration with Jacob & Co. — feature miniature Ronaldo figures in iconic poses, his No.7 in gold on the case back and movements decorated with his signature. They range from $29,000 to $160,000 and represent the most commercially successful watch collaboration between a sportsman and a watchmaker in recent history.

WHAT RONALDO'S COLLECTION TELLS US

Ronaldo's watch choices reflect his broader aesthetic: maximum visibility, extreme craftsmanship, personal branding and no apology for excess. Unlike some celebrity collectors who have migrated toward understated Swiss dress watches as they mature, Ronaldo has doubled down on scale, diamonds and spectacle.

This is not unsophisticated. The Girard-Perregaux Planetarium and the Franck Muller invisible setting both represent genuine horological achievement. But the aesthetic priority is always visual impact over collector credibility — which is entirely consistent with someone who has spent his career performing on the world's largest stages for the world's largest audiences.

For the watch world, Ronaldo's collection matters because his 600 million Instagram followers notice what he wears. Every post featuring a Jacob & Co. or a diamond Rolex introduces millions of people to luxury watchmaking who would never have found it through Hodinkee or Revolution Magazine. That audience is the future of the industry.
