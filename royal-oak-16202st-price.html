The Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Jumbo 16202ST is the current-production 39mm Extra-Thin Royal Oak in steel — the watch that, in 2022, succeeded the legendary 15202ST as AP marked its 150th anniversary. Its official retail price in 2026 is about US$40,100 (roughly £33,000 in the UK), but it is boutique-only and waitlisted, so on the secondary market the 16202ST trades far higher — around £80,000–£130,000. Here’s what that premium actually reflects.

What the 16202ST is

The 16202ST is the modern Jumbo: the same slim 39mm Extra-Thin silhouette that started the Royal Oak story in 1972, now with the updated Calibre 7121 automatic movement. Launched in 2022 for AP’s 150th anniversary, it carries subtle "50 Years" detailing and replaced the outgoing 15202. It is the reference you are actually quoted on at retail today — which, given AP’s allocation model, is more theory than practice for most buyers.

Retail price in 2026

The 16202ST’s official retail price in 2026 is approximately US$40,100 (around £33,000 in the UK). But like every steel Royal Oak, it is sold boutique-only and allocated to established clients by waitlist — so almost nobody buys one at list. That gap between list and reality is exactly why the secondary market matters here.

Secondary market price in 2026

On the secondary market the 16202ST trades at roughly £80,000–£130,000 (about US$104,000–$169,000) in 2026 — well above retail. Crucially, that premium is driven by scarcity and waitlists, not the price of metal: unlike gold sports watches, which have felt real cost pressure with gold near record highs, the steel Jumbo’s value moves on demand alone. When you read a "16202ST market price," you are reading a scarcity number. Condition, box and papers, and the week all move it, so confirm the live figure for the exact watch.

The steel Jumbo has been called overpriced at every price point since 1972. The collectors who ignored that have never regretted it.

How it compares

The 16202ST is the current-production Jumbo; its discontinued predecessor, the 15202ST, trades at a comparable £75,000–£110,000 as a closed-production collectible. Both sit far above the 41mm 15510ST Selfwinding (~£30,000–£38,000). The quick answers

What is the retail price of the Royal Oak 16202ST in 2026?
The Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Jumbo 16202ST has an official 2026 retail price of approximately US$40,100 (around £33,000 in the UK). Because it is boutique-only and waitlisted, almost no buyer actually purchases one at that list price.
What does the 16202ST trade for on the secondary market?
In 2026 the Royal Oak Jumbo 16202ST trades at roughly £80,000–£130,000 (about US$104,000–$169,000) on the secondary market — far above retail. The premium is a scarcity number driven by demand and waitlists, not the price of metal, which is why the steel Jumbo holds value more steadily than gold references.
What is the difference between the 15202ST and the 16202ST?
The 15202ST (discontinued 2022) and the 16202ST (2022–present) are both the 39mm Extra-Thin steel Jumbo. The 15202 used the historic ultra-thin Calibre 2121; the current 16202 uses the updated Calibre 7121 and carries 150th-anniversary detailing. The 15202 now trades as a closed-production collectible; the 16202 is current production, still technically orderable.
Is the Royal Oak 16202ST a good buy in 2026?
For the collector who wants the current, in-production steel Jumbo, yes — it is the most iconic silhouette in the family and its premium has held steadily on scarcity. But it is a large outlay well above retail, so knowing the fair market figure for the exact example, and its condition and papers, matters more than the list price you'll never be offered.
