Grails, not gifts

When Royal Challengers Bengaluru lifted a second IPL title in 2026, the cameras caught Virat Kohli mid-celebration — and the watch world caught something else. On his wrist, through the confetti and the dancing, was a Patek Philippe Aquanaut Travel Time 5164G, a white-gold piece reported at around ₹1.6 crore (Free Press Journal). For most people that is a house. For Kohli, it is one watch in a collection that quietly ranks among the most serious in Indian sport.

The moment

The ₹1.6 crore watch at the IPL 2026 party

A white-gold Patek, hiding in plain sight through the celebrations.

The watch eagle-eyed fans identified on Kohli’s wrist during RCB’s 2026 title celebration was the Patek Philippe Aquanaut Travel Time 5164G — a dual-time-zone Aquanaut in white gold, with the signature embossed dial in blue-grey, a 40.8mm case and a matching composite strap. Indian coverage reported its value at around ₹1.6 crore (Free Press Journal).

What makes the choice telling is how understated it is. White gold reads, from a distance, like steel — so unless you know the reference, you miss it entirely. That is the collector’s version of flexing: a watch that says everything to the handful of people who recognise it, and nothing to everyone else. It is also squarely in Patek territory, the maison whose steel and precious-metal sports watches have led the modern market.

The collection

What else is in the box

Patek, Rolex and Audemars Piguet — several of them grails in their own right.

The Aquanaut is not an outlier. Over the years, Kohli has been photographed in and reported to own a run of watches that most serious collectors spend a lifetime chasing — and the spread tells you he (or whoever advises him) actually knows watches. The headline piece is a Rolex Cosmograph Daytona “Rainbow” in Everose gold, its bezel set with sapphires in a graduated spectrum, reported at around ₹4.6 crore (India.com). It is one of the most coveted — and allocation-only — watches Rolex makes; we told the story of what a Rainbow Daytona commands at auction when one set a national record earlier this year.

Why it matters

What Kohli’s wrist says about India

The most-watched man in the country is also one of its most serious collectors.

Kohli is, by most measures, India’s most valuable celebrity — so what he wears sets a reference point. A decade ago, the aspirational Indian watch was a steel Rolex. Today the country’s biggest sportsman is in white-gold Pateks and a Rainbow Daytona, and millions notice. That is the same current we keep tracking: a Lord Shiva grail built for the Indian market, watchmaking’s Oscars touring Mumbai, and India being taken seriously as a watch culture rather than just a place that buys watches.

It also quietly moves markets. When a reference lands on the right wrist, demand follows — and the pieces Kohli favours (independent-adjacent Pateks, allocation-only Rolexes) are exactly the ones that already hold or gain value in the 2026 market.

Questions, answered

The quick answers

What watch did Virat Kohli wear at the IPL 2026 celebration?

During RCB’s 2026 IPL title celebration, Kohli was identified wearing a Patek Philippe Aquanaut Travel Time 5164G — a white-gold, dual-time-zone Aquanaut reported at around ₹1.6 crore by Indian media (Free Press Journal).

What is the most expensive watch Virat Kohli owns?

The most valuable piece reported in his collection is a Rolex Cosmograph Daytona “Rainbow” in Everose gold, with a sapphire-set bezel, reported at around ₹4.6 crore. It is one of the rarest, allocation-only watches Rolex produces.

What watches are in Virat Kohli’s collection?

Reported pieces include a Rolex Daytona “Rainbow,” a Patek Philippe Perpetual Calendar, the Patek Aquanaut Travel Time 5164G, an Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Perpetual Calendar, a Patek Philippe Nautilus and a steel Rolex Daytona “Panda.” The collection spans Patek Philippe, Rolex and Audemars Piguet.

How much is Virat Kohli’s watch collection worth?

There is no official figure, and reported values vary by source. Based on Indian media estimates, the headline pieces alone — the Rainbow Daytona, the green Patek Perpetual Calendar and the Aquanaut — add up to well over ₹8 crore.
