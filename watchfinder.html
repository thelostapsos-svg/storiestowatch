How We Verify Sources

✦

Manually Curated

Every source is hand-selected and individually reviewed on merit — never automated scraping.

✦

Checked Against Primary Sources

Each dealer is checked against public, primary sources — official presence, stated dealer or trade status, and established trading reputation — before listing.

✦

Honestly Categorised

Authorised dealers, grey-market specialists, online platforms and auction houses — each labelled for exactly what it is, so you always know who you're dealing with.

✦

Reviewed & Current

Listings are reviewed monthly, with sources added or removed as standing changes — never left to go stale.

A note on verification. Stories To Watch is an independent discovery platform — we curate and review the sources we list, but we don't sell, hold, authenticate, or take part in any sale. Our checks are based on public information, not a guarantee of any dealer or watch. Any purchase is between you and the dealer, so always do your own due diligence.
