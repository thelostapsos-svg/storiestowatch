The Audemars Piguet Royal Oak 15510ST is the current-generation 41mm Royal Oak Selfwinding in steel — the reference you are actually quoted on at an AP boutique today. Its official retail price in 2026 is around £23,500 in the UK (US$31,900 in the States), but AP’s boutique-only, waitlist model means very few buyers get one at that number. On the secondary market the 15510ST trades at roughly £30,000–£38,000 — about 1.3–1.6× retail. Here’s exactly what that buys, and how to get it at a fair price.

What the 15510ST actually is

Introduced in 2023, the 15510ST replaced the 15500ST as the standard 41mm Royal Oak Selfwinding. It keeps the same 41mm steel case and in-house automatic Calibre 4302, but the Grande Tapisserie dial now runs edge-to-edge with no separate printed minute track, the “AP” monogram is replaced by the full Audemars Piguet signature, and the bracelet links are slimmer — a subtle nod to the 15202 Jumbo. It is more accessible than the Jumbo and more wearable than the complicated references, which is exactly why it’s the most-searched Royal Oak after the Jumbo.

Retail price in 2026 — and why you probably can’t buy at it

The 15510ST’s official retail price in 2026 is approximately £23,500 at UK boutiques and US$31,900 in the United States. But Audemars Piguet has moved to a boutique-only model — it no longer works with multi-brand authorised dealers, and steel sports references are allocated to established clients through waitlists. In practice, walking into an AP House and buying a 15510ST at list is very rare, which is why most buyers turn to the secondary market. Secondary market price in 2026

On the secondary market, the 15510ST trades at roughly £30,000–£38,000 (about US$40,000–$50,000) in 2026 — a premium of around 1.3–1.6× retail. That premium reflects genuine, current demand and the waitlist reality, not speculation: the wider Royal Oak market corrected from its early-2022 peak (the predecessor 15500 hit ~2.5× retail then) and has normalised to healthier levels. Condition, box and papers, and dial all move the number, so always confirm the live figure for the exact watch before you commit.

Retail is the number on the list. The market price is the number you’ll actually pay — knowing the gap is the whole game.

What is the difference between the 15500ST and the 15510ST?
Both are the 41mm Royal Oak Selfwinding with the Calibre 4302. The 15500ST (2019–2023) has a separate printed minute track and the "AP" monogram; the current 15510ST (2023–present) has an edge-to-edge Grande Tapisserie dial, the full "Audemars Piguet" signature, and slimmer bracelet links. The 15510 is current production; the 15500 now trades as a recently-superseded reference.
