Cannes Film Festival 2026

THE CROISETTE
Watch Report

Fourteen extraordinary timepieces. One red carpet. The 79th Cannes Film Festival doubled as the world's most glamorous watch exhibition.

23 May 2026 · 14 Watches Spotted · Stories To Watch Editorial

Every May, the world's most powerful people descend on the French Riviera — and the cameras don't just watch the films. The 79th Cannes Film Festival was, as ever, a masterclass in dressed-up wealth. Audemars Piguet dominated the wrist game, appearing on four separate celebrities. Rolex, Chopard and Cartier all made their presence felt. And in among the jewellery-watch category, a few genuine collector pieces appeared that will be talked about long after the Palme d'Or is handed out. Here is every significant watch we spotted on the Croisette — and what each one says about the person wearing it.

14 Timepieces · 79th Cannes Film Festival
01

Bella Hadid

Rolex Lady-Datejust Vintage — Yellow Gold

While Chopard dressed most of the women at Cannes in new high jewellery, Hadid chose a vintage Rolex Lady-Datejust in yellow gold — a deliberate counter-statement. Vintage Rolex in yellow gold is having a serious collector moment right now, driven partly by a generation that's rejecting the stainless steel sports watch orthodoxy. The Lady-Datejust in gold is understated, feminine and deeply considered. At Cannes, surrounded by maximalist jewellery, it stood out precisely because it didn't try.

02

Vin Diesel

Audemars Piguet CODE 11.59 "Star Wheel"

The CODE 11.59 Star Wheel is one of AP's most polarising pieces — loved by collectors for its wandering hours complication, questioned by purists for departing from Royal Oak orthodoxy. Diesel's choice of the Star Wheel rather than a Royal Oak reads as a deliberate statement of independence. The wandering hours complication — where the time is displayed by numerals that rotate around the dial in a star formation — is genuinely spectacular to watch in person. Diesel understands that not everything needs to be a Submariner.

03

Vincent Cassel

Audemars Piguet Royal Oak "Neo Frame" — Rose Gold

The veteran French actor closed out AP's Cannes dominance with the Royal Oak Neo Frame in rose gold — a recent evolution of the Royal Oak that replaces the traditional octagonal bezel with a more architecturally abstracted case geometry. Cassel has been an AP ambassador for years and knows the collection intimately. The Neo Frame is newer territory for the brand and its appearance on a serious collector like Cassel signals that AP's design evolution is being endorsed from within its own community.

04

Ella Beau Travolta

Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Mini Frosted Gold — White Gold

The Royal Oak Mini in Frosted Gold — a collaboration between AP and Florentine jeweller Carolina Bucci — gives the Royal Oak's iconic integrated bracelet a hammered gold finish that scatters light like no other luxury watch on the market. In white gold, the effect is ethereal. At Cannes, it confirmed what collectors already know: the Frosted Gold Royal Oak Mini is the definitive women's luxury watch of this decade.

05

Tamara Kalinic

Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Mini Frosted Gold — Yellow Gold

The fashion influencer wore the yellow gold version of the Royal Oak Mini Frosted Gold — same Carolina Bucci collaboration as Ella Beau Travolta, different metal. The warmth of yellow gold against the hammered texture creates a different mood entirely — richer and more maximalist. Two people wearing the same reference at the same event would normally be a faux pas, but the Royal Oak Mini Frosted in different metals reads as completely distinct pieces. That is the measure of good design.

06

Sebastian Stan

Cartier Roadster — Yellow Gold

The Marvel actor chose a Cartier Roadster in yellow gold — a relatively rare choice that signals genuine watch knowledge. The Roadster was discontinued in the late 2000s and has been building a quiet cult following among collectors who recognise it as one of Cartier's most successfully sporty references. In yellow gold, it is genuinely uncommon on the red carpet, which is presumably why Stan chose it — the man who plays Bucky Barnes clearly knows his way around a grey market list.

07

Colman Domingo

Omega Seamaster Aqua Terra 30mm — Steel & Diamonds

The two-time Oscar nominee and Boucheron jewellery ambassador made a bracelet choice that crossed categories — the Omega Seamaster Aqua Terra at 30mm with a diamond-set dial and case. At Cannes, where male celebrities typically default to oversized sport watches, a diamond-set Omega at this scale is a statement of intentional elegance. Domingo has always used fashion as self-expression, and this watch fits that philosophy perfectly.

Find Omega References
08

Odessa A'zion

Chopard Happy Sport Tourbillon Joaillerie

The Happy Sport Tourbillon Joaillerie combines Chopard's most recognisable signature — the seven freely floating diamonds on the dial — with a tourbillon complication in a jewellery-grade case. The floating diamonds moving freely under the sapphire crystal remain one of the most genuinely playful ideas in fine watchmaking. At Cannes, A'zion wore it as an extension of a maximalist fashion moment, and it delivered.

09

Connor Swindells

Chopard L.U.C. XPS — Rose Gold & Diamonds

Bridgerton's leading man chose Chopard's most horologically serious offering — the L.U.C. XPS, a manually-wound ultra-thin movement in a case dressed with diamonds. L.U.C. is Chopard's manufacture movement division, and the XPS is its thinnest expression. At just over 3mm thick, wearing it under a white tie cuff is an exercise in invisible luxury. The diamond setting at Cannes means this particular piece is very much a red carpet-specific configuration.

10

Marion Cotillard

Chanel Gardenia Première — Yellow Gold & Diamonds

The Chanel ambassador wore the Gardenia Première, where the watch face is shaped like the iconic Chanel No. 5 bottle stopper — the same form that has defined the Première line since Coco Chanel first sketched it. In yellow gold with diamonds, it is one of the most recognisable luxury watch silhouettes in the world. Cotillard wearing Chanel at Cannes is expected, but the choice of the Gardenia over the more common J12 was considered.

11

Dua Lipa

Bulgari Mysterious Serpents High Jewellery

Lipa wore what is technically a watch but functions as a jewellery sculpture — Bulgari's Serpenti Mysterious, where the watch dial is hidden within the snake's body and revealed only when you know where to look. The Serpenti line traces back to Bulgari's 1940s coiled snake bracelets, and the Mysterious complications have been among the most coveted pieces at auction. On Lipa, it worked as a theatrical extension of Cannes' characteristic excess.

12

Carlos Sainz

Girard-Perregaux Tourbillon With Three Flying Bridges — Rose Gold

The Ferrari F1 driver brought the most technically spectacular piece of the festival — the GP Tourbillon with Three Flying Bridges, a watch built around one of the most visually arresting movements in Swiss watchmaking. The three gold bridges that support the gear train are the centrepiece of the dial, turning mechanical engineering into art. In rose gold, the price sits around £120,000–£150,000. For Sainz, it's a fitting choice — a man who spends his life at the intersection of precision machinery and spectacle.

13

Gabrielle Caunesil

Piaget Swinging Sautoir — Rose Gold, Malachite, Diamonds & Emeralds

The French model wore a piece from Piaget's haute joaillerie archive — the Swinging Sautoir, a long pendant watch that belongs to the category of wearable art. Malachite, diamonds and emeralds in combination with rose gold is a deeply 1970s Piaget palette, and its appearance at Cannes 2026 signals a clear directional shift toward vintage-inflected luxury at the very top of the market.

14

Adam Driver

Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Perpetual Calendar — Sand Gold

The most serious collector piece of the entire festival. Driver chose the Royal Oak Perpetual Calendar in sand gold — a complication-heavy watch that displays the day, date, month, moon phase and leap year cycle simultaneously on a dial that never needs manual adjustment until 2100. Sand gold is AP's proprietary 18-carat alloy, warmer than rose and rarer in the market. Retail price: approximately £120,000. This is not a watch you buy to be seen — it's a watch you buy because you understand what it took to build it.

The Croisette in 2026: AP's Red Carpet

Audemars Piguet appeared on five wrists at Cannes 2026 — more than any other brand, by some distance. The Royal Oak's dominance of the luxury watch conversation continues, but the more interesting story is how AP appeared: not just Royal Oaks, but Perpetual Calendars, CODE 11.59 complications and Neo Frames. The brand's roster is diversifying, and the Croisette reflected that. The other defining trend was yellow gold's quiet return — Bella Hadid's vintage Datejust and Tamara Kalinic's Frosted Royal Oak both made the case for gold as the collector metal of the moment. And quietly, Carlos Sainz's Girard-Perregaux was the most technically extraordinary piece of the week — a reminder that outside the big three, exceptional watchmaking is very much alive.
