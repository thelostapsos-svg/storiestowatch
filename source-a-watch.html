Questions, answered

What to know before you ask us to find it.

How does the Stories To Watch concierge work?

You tell us the exact watch — reference, budget, timeframe and how flexible you are on year, condition or pre-owned. We give you an honest read on whether it's realistically attainable and at what, search the right channels — retail, pre-owned, grey market and auction — and come back with the genuine routes and our read on each. You buy directly from the dealer; we never take the watch or your money.

What does the watch concierge cost?

A success fee, agreed in writing upfront and charged only once you approve a watch: $500 under $10,000, $1,500 up to $50,000, 3% above that, and 2.5% plus bespoke terms at the grail tier. There is no cost to inquire, and no markup hidden in the price.

Is it safe? Do you hold my money or the watch?

No — we never take custody of the watch or your funds. Payment is directly between you and the dealer. We find the piece, verify it and guide the deal, but you own every decision and every approval. Every dealer we work with is verified and publicly listed in our Watch Finder.

How long does it take to source a watch?

It depends entirely on the piece. A liquid, current reference can surface in days; a discontinued or pre-owned grail can take weeks of watching the right channels; a hard allocation piece may not be realistically obtainable soon at all. If so, we tell you that at the start — along with the smarter route — rather than promise a timeline we cannot keep.

What watches can you source?

Most references that genuinely trade on the market — new, pre-owned, vintage or discontinued — across Rolex, Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, and on to independents and Grand Seiko. Some pieces are only realistic pre-owned; some allocation-driven references simply can't be bought on demand. Our job is to tell you honestly which is which, and guide you to the smartest available route.

How is this different from other watch sourcing services?

We work buyer-side only. We hold no inventory, so we never steer you into our own stock — there is none. Our network isn't a secret list; every dealer is publicly visible in our Watch Finder. And we never hold the watch or your money — you buy direct. Most concierges are dealers or marketplaces with a markup; we are a guide that works for you, not a seller.
