On 5 October 2026 — James Bond Day, the anniversary of Dr. No’s 1962 premiere — Omega unveiled a Seamaster Diver 300M for James Bond and, for the first time, one for his nemesis, Spectre — a collection it frames as “Timeless Adversaries.” The headline pair are steel at $8,600 each; the rivalry climbs all the way up into platinum-gold limited sets. Same 42mm case, same movement, opposite souls — and Omega is asking you, only half-jokingly, to choose which side you’re on.

What happened

Two Seamasters, one rivalry

Omega put the hero and the villain on matching wrists — and made them a pair.

The two watches are both the Seamaster Diver 300M, the model that has been James Bond’s watch on screen since 1995. In steel, they are mechanically identical: a 42mm case, the Master Chronometer Calibre 8806, 300m of water resistance, a helium-escape valve at 10 o’clock, a 55-hour power reserve and a mesh (“Milanese”) bracelet (Monochrome).

The difference is all character. The “James Bond” edition wears a blue dial with a titanium bezel ring — cool, official, the good guy. The “Spectre” edition answers with a grained black dial and a black-ceramic bezel — the shadow version, named for the criminal organisation Bond has spent sixty years fighting (SJX). Each steel watch sells for $8,600.

The story

Why it’s a Seamaster — and why that matters

Bond has worn an Omega for thirty years. This is the first time his enemy got one too.

The Seamaster became Bond’s watch in 1995’s GoldenEye, when costume designer Lindy Hemming put a Seamaster Quartz Professional on Pierce Brosnan — reasoning that a modern naval-intelligence officer would wear a diver, not a dress watch. Omega has outfitted every Bond since, through the entire Daniel Craig era, and the Seamaster Diver 300M is now as much a part of the character as the Walther and the Aston Martin.

What makes the 2026 collection a genuine first is the Spectre half. Omega has made Bond-themed Seamasters before — most famously the 2015 Spectre film edition — but it has never built a watch for the other side. Turning a sixty-year rivalry into a matched pair of watches is a clever piece of storytelling, and exactly the kind of culture-first grail the market now rewards — the same current we tracked in the 2026 market read.

Most brands sell you the hero. Omega just sold you the villain too — and dared you to admit which one you’d rather be.

Questions, answered

The quick answers

What is the Omega James Bond and Spectre Seamaster collection?

It is a pair of Omega Seamaster Diver 300M editions launched on 5 October 2026 (James Bond Day), presented as “Timeless Adversaries”: a blue-dial “James Bond” edition and a black-dial “Spectre” edition, representing 007 and the criminal organisation he fights.

How much do the Omega Bond and Spectre Seamasters cost?

Each steel edition is priced at $8,600. Each side also comes as a 77-numbered limited set at $89,800 — a steel watch paired with a 42mm platinum-gold version of the same edition, sold only as a set, not separately.

What is the difference between the Bond and Spectre editions?

They are mechanically identical — a 42mm steel case, Master Chronometer Calibre 8806, 300m water resistance and a mesh bracelet. The difference is the look: the Bond edition has a blue dial with a titanium bezel ring, while the Spectre edition has a grained black dial with a black-ceramic bezel.

Has Omega always been James Bond’s watch?

Since 1995. Costume designer Lindy Hemming put an Omega Seamaster on Pierce Brosnan in GoldenEye, and Omega has supplied every James Bond watch since, through the Daniel Craig era. Before 1995, Bond was most associated with Rolex.
